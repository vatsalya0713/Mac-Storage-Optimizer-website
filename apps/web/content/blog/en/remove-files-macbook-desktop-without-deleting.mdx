A cluttered Desktop doesn't just look messy — every icon on it is a file macOS has to render and index, and it's an easy place to lose track of what's actually important. Here's how to clear it without risking anything you need.

## Step 1: Sort before you touch anything

In Finder, open your Desktop folder and switch to list view sorted by **Date Modified**. This immediately separates recent work (keep visible) from files you haven't touched in months (safe to move).

## Step 2: Use Stacks instead of deleting

Right-click the Desktop and choose **Use Stacks**. macOS automatically groups files by kind (documents, images, PDFs) without moving or deleting anything — often enough to make a cluttered Desktop feel manageable again with zero risk.

## Step 3: Move, don't delete, anything you're unsure about

For files you don't need visible but aren't ready to remove permanently:

- **Drag them into a dated folder** like `~/Documents/Desktop Archive 2026`, out of sight but still fully intact.
- **Move them to iCloud Drive or an external drive** if they're large and rarely accessed — this frees local space without deleting the file at all.

## Step 4: Only delete what you're certain about

Once genuinely unimportant files are isolated (old screenshots, installer `.dmg` files you already ran, duplicate downloads), that's the point to actually delete — and even then, moving to Trash first (the default) keeps a safety net.
