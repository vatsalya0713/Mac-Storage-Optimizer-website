You can [download MacDiskCleaner](/downloads) straight from this site — no email sign-up, no trial countdown. Here’s what you get and how to install it properly.

## What’s included free

- A full scan of your Mac (or the folders you choose): junk and caches, duplicates, large and old files, old downloads, developer data and app leftovers.
- Every result visible — nothing hidden behind a paywall.
- Up to **2 GB of cleanup in total**, for free, permanently.
- Trash-first deletion, so anything you remove can be put back.

Pro ($12.99 once) removes the 2 GB limit and adds an app uninstaller, privacy cleaner, startup manager and scheduled scans. [Compare free and Pro](/pricing).

## System requirements

- macOS 14 Sonoma or later
- Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) or Intel processor
- About 50 MB of free space

## Option 1: Install with no warning (Terminal)

Open **Terminal** (Applications → Utilities), paste this line and press Return:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://www.macdiskcleaner.com/install.sh | bash
```

It downloads the latest version, checks its published SHA-256 checksum, installs it in Applications and opens it. You can [read the script](/install.sh) before running it.

## Option 2: Download the .dmg

1. Click **Download** on the [downloads page](/downloads) and open **MacDiskCleaner.dmg**.
2. Drag **MacDiskCleaner** into the **Applications** folder.
3. Open it from Applications. macOS shows “Apple could not verify…” — click **Done** (never **Move to Bin**).
4. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to MacDiskCleaner, then confirm with your password.

That check only happens once. On macOS 14 Sonoma you can instead right-click the app and choose **Open**.

## Is it safe to download Mac cleaners?

It is when you get them from the developer’s own site and they behave transparently. MacDiskCleaner:

- **Never uploads** file names, paths or contents — scanning is local.
- **Moves everything to the Trash** instead of erasing it.
- **Blocks protected locations** such as system folders, Keychains and Preferences.
- **Publishes a SHA-256 checksum** for every release on the [safety page](/security).

Avoid third-party download sites that wrap apps in their own installers.

## After installing

Run your first scan from the Dashboard. You can choose a quick scan (without the slower duplicate check) or a full one, and pick which folders to include. Results are saved, so you don’t have to scan again after reopening the app.
