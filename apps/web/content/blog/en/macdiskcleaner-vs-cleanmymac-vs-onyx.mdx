These three tools solve the same underlying problem — a Mac accumulating junk, duplicates, and forgotten large files — but take very different approaches. Here's how they actually compare.

## At a glance

| | MacDiskCleaner | CleanMyMac X | OnyX |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | Free tier + $12.99 one-time Pro | Subscription | Free |
| Interface | Guided, beginner-friendly | Polished, beginner-friendly | Technical, manual |
| Duplicate finder | Yes | Yes | No |
| App uninstaller (with leftovers) | Yes | Yes | No |
| Large file scanner | Yes | Yes | Manual only |
| Scheduled automatic scans | Yes (Pro) | Yes | No |
| Review before delete | Yes, everything goes to Trash first | Yes | Varies by task |

## MacDiskCleaner

The core pitch is simple: no subscription. You pay once for Pro ($12.99) or use the free tier indefinitely (up to 2 GB of junk cleanup, plus a full duplicate and large-file scanner with no time limit). It's built specifically for macOS with native APIs, so it stays light on system resources rather than running background processes constantly.

**Choose this if:** you want the full guided-cleanup experience without a recurring charge.

## CleanMyMac X

The most feature-rich of the three, with modules covering everything from malware scanning to optimization. The tradeoff is the subscription model — useful if you want continuous protection and don't mind the ongoing cost, less appealing if you just want an occasional deep clean.

**Choose this if:** you want the broadest feature set and are fine paying for it every year.

## OnyX

A free macOS maintenance utility that's been around for years, covering cache clearing, permission verification, and system maintenance tasks. It has no guided "what's safe to delete" experience — it assumes you already understand what each maintenance task does, which makes it a better fit for technically confident users than for someone just trying to free up space quickly.

**Choose this if:** you're comfortable with manual system maintenance and want a free, no-frills tool.

## The bottom line

If subscription fatigue is the deciding factor for you — which is the single most common reason people search for a CleanMyMac alternative — MacDiskCleaner's one-time pricing is the most direct answer. [Try the free tier](/free-mac-cleaner) and see how much space it recovers before deciding whether Pro is worth it for your setup.
