Running low on disk space is one of the most common Mac complaints — and one of the easiest to fix, if you pick the right tool. We tested the most-searched Mac storage cleaner apps of 2026 across three things that actually matter: how much space they recover, how safe their deletions are, and what they cost long-term.

## What we looked for

A good Mac storage cleaner should:

- **Never delete without asking.** Files should go to Trash first, not vanish permanently.
- **Find real junk**, not just flag system files that macOS still needs.
- **Be honest about pricing** — a lot of "free" cleaners gate the actual cleanup behind a subscription.

**Best for:** advanced users comfortable with manual system maintenance.

## FAQ

**Do I need to pay to clean junk files on my Mac?**
No. Every cleaner on this list has some free functionality. MacDiskCleaner's free tier covers up to 2 GB of junk cleanup with no time limit.

**Is it safe to let a cleaner app delete system files?**
Only if it moves files to Trash first and clearly separates "safe to remove" junk from files macOS actually needs. Avoid any tool that permanently deletes without a review step.
