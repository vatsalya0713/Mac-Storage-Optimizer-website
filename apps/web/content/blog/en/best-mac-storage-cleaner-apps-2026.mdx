“Mac cleaner” covers very different tools: guided all-in-one suites, disk visualisers and maintenance toolboxes. Here’s how the main options compare, so you can pick the right kind first.

## How we compared them

- **Price model** — free, one-time or subscription.
- **What it finds** — junk, duplicates, large files, app leftovers.
- **Safety** — does it show items first and use the Trash?
- **Ease of use** — is it guided or manual?

We looked at each developer’s published features and pricing in October 2026.

## Quick comparison

| App | Type | Price | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| MacDiskCleaner | Guided storage cleaner | Free tier + $12.99 once | Getting space back without a subscription |
| CleanMyMac | All-in-one suite | Subscription (≈$39.95/yr) or one-time licence | Widest feature set incl. malware scanning |
| DaisyDisk | Disk visualiser | $9.99 once | Exploring your disk visually |
| OnyX | Maintenance toolbox | Free | Advanced users, maintenance scripts |
| GrandPerspective | Disk visualiser | Free | A free treemap of your disk |
| AppCleaner | Uninstaller | Free | Removing apps and their leftovers |

## MacDiskCleaner

A native, guided storage cleaner. One scan finds junk and caches, duplicates, large and old files, old downloads, developer data (Xcode, `node_modules`, package caches) and app leftovers; you choose what goes and everything is moved to the Trash first. **Free:** unlimited scanning and up to 2 GB of cleanup. **Pro:** $12.99 one-time for unlimited cleanup, uninstaller, privacy cleaner, startup manager and scheduled scans.

**Not included:** malware scanning or a visual disk map.

## CleanMyMac

MacPaw’s polished suite combines cleanup, malware scanning, performance tools, an uninstaller and an app updater. Excellent for people who want everything in one place. Mostly sold as a yearly subscription; 7-day trial. [Compare it with MacDiskCleaner](/vs/cleanmymac).

## DaisyDisk

DaisyDisk’s interactive sunburst map is the nicest way to see what fills your disk; you then delete items yourself. One-time $9.99 for up to five personal Macs; the trial scans but can’t delete. [Compare it with MacDiskCleaner](/vs/daisydisk).

## OnyX

A long-standing free utility for maintenance scripts, cache clearing and hidden settings — powerful but technical, with no duplicate or large-file finder. [Compare it with MacDiskCleaner](/vs/onyx).

## GrandPerspective and AppCleaner

Two respected free single-purpose tools: GrandPerspective draws a treemap of disk usage, and AppCleaner removes apps along with their support files. Both are covered in our [best free Mac cleaners list](/blog/best-free-mac-cleaner-apps-2026).

## Our picks

- **Most people:** MacDiskCleaner — start free, pay once only if you need more.
- **Want everything, including malware tools:** CleanMyMac.
- **Visual thinkers:** DaisyDisk, optionally paired with a guided cleaner.
- **Power users on a budget:** OnyX plus GrandPerspective.

## What to avoid

Skip any “cleaner” that claims your Mac is infected, promises to speed it up by “cleaning RAM”, or deletes files without showing them first. macOS already manages memory and system caches itself.
